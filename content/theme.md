The terminal rotates through twelve non-green color palettes across the 24-hour clock (one station every two hours). Each palette sets three CSS custom properties (`--background`, `--foreground`, and `--accent`); secondary UI tokens (`--dim` and `--line`) and the 3D wireframe game's colors are derived automatically from those three.
