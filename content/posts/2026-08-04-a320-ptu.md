Two of the A320's hydraulic systems, green and yellow, are joined by one shaft
through a sealed bulkhead: a bent-axis piston motor on one end, a pump on the
other. No fluid crosses. When the pressure gap between them reaches about
500 psi the shaft breaks away and spins up, and that spool-up is the barking you
hear under the wing at the gate.

I built a simulator: [the barking dog](/sim/a320-ptu/). Drop an engine pump
offline, drag the flight-control demand, and watch the differential cross 500.
Turn the sound on.

Flow rates and time constants are tuned for legibility; the numbers are
illustrative.
